### ⚡ About Me

- 🛠️ **Software & Systems:** Architecting web platforms and authentication systems (**VOLQ**), container deployments (**OpenWebUI / OmniRoute**), and school management platforms (**KMSC & ConnectEd**).
- 🎙️ **Low-Level & Audio:** Building **VOLQ-MIC**—an ultra-low-latency phone-to-PC audio streaming architecture using **Rust, Tauri 2, Vue 3, and raw UDP/TCP**.
- 🤖 **Hardware & Embedded:** Prototyping sensor arrays and IoT systems with **Arduino Uno** and **ESP32-CAM** (including an automated flood-level & soil-moisture monitor with *Team Helix*).
- ✈️ **Aeromodeling & Avionics:** Scratch-building a 1000mm wingspan Depron **Sukhoi Su-57 Felon** RC jet to test 64mm EDF vs. pusher-propulsion aerodynamics.
- 🐧 **OS & Optimization:** Tweaking Linux kernels (Arch / CachyOS / Fedora), Android GSIs/ADB optimizations, and custom Minecraft client mods.
